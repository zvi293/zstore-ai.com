Why work with a one-person studio?

Because the person you brief is the person who builds it. Your words don't get rewritten on the way from the first call to the code, and you always know who is responsible.

  1. A

    One accountable person

    If something isn't right, you never have to find out whose job it was. It was mine, and I fix it.

  2. B

    You say it once

    You explain your business to the person doing the work. No relays, no summaries of summaries.

  3. C

    Design and code from one head

    The details you approve in the design are the details that ship, because nobody has to interpret them.

  4. D

    Honest, focused capacity

    One person can't run ten projects at once, so I don't. Start dates are scheduled and scope is agreed up front, and once we start, your project gets my full attention.

What do I believe about websites?

Five principles behind every project. Each one fits in a sentence, and each one comes with an example you can check.

  • 01

    Start with a real conversation.

    The best brief is a talk, not a form.

    For example “We need a new website” often turns out to mean “we need more booked calls”, and that changes the whole plan.

  • 02

    Mobile first, always.

    Most visitors arrive on a phone, so I design for a small screen before a big one.

    For example every button on this page is at least 44 pixels tall, so a thumb can hit it.

  • 03

    Fast is a feature.

    A page that loads quickly feels trustworthy before anyone reads a word.

    For example this site is hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks or page builders in the way.

  • 04

    Accessible by default.

    A website should work for everyone, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader or larger text.

    For example the switch above works with the Tab and Space keys, and it announces what changed.

  • 05

    No templates, no tricks.

    Search and AI visibility come from clear content and solid foundations, not hacks.

    For example I tell you which AI search optimization steps are proven and which are cheap bets, before you spend time on them.

Is Zstore AI really just one person?

Yes. Zstore AI is my studio and I'm the only person in it. I design, code, test and launch every project myself, and I'm the one who answers your emails and messages. That's a deliberate choice: it keeps every decision and detail in one pair of hands.

What happens if you're sick or unavailable?

Timelines in your proposal include realistic buffers, and you'll always hear from me directly if anything affects the schedule. Your site is also built on open web standards rather than a proprietary platform, so any capable developer can read and work on the code.

Which languages do you work in?

I work with clients in English and Hebrew. Projects for US, UK, European and other international clients run in English, with clear written updates so nothing gets lost between time zones.

What kinds of clients do you usually work with?

Founders, small and mid-size businesses, e-commerce brands, professionals and startups, mostly people who want a website that is genuinely better than a template. The common thread is a business that cares how it comes across and wants one accountable person to build it.
