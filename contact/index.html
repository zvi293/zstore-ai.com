01Before you write

What should I include in my first message?

Three things: what you're building, who it's for, and when you'd like it live. That's enough for a useful first reply.

  1. 01

    What you're building

    A new site, an online store, a landing page or a redesign. One sentence is fine.

  2. 02

    Who it's for

    The people you want to reach, and the one thing you'd like them to do.

  3. 03

    When you'd like it live

    A launch date, an event to be ready for, or simply “soon”.

Nice to have, never required

  • Sites you like, and why
  • Your current site
  • Content you already have
  • A budget range you're comfortable with

Want to go further? My guide to writing a website brief has a template, and the pricing page explains how the fixed price works.

4 quick answers

Something else you'd like to know first?

Email me directly →
Do I need a finished brief before I contact you?

No. A few sentences about what you're building and why is enough to start. If you'd like to be more prepared, my guide to writing a website brief has a template, but working out the details together is part of the first conversation.

Is WhatsApp or email better?

Whichever you prefer. Email suits longer briefs and attachments; WhatsApp suits a quick first hello or a voice note. Both reach me directly, and I reply within one business day either way.

Can we talk on a video call?

Yes. Most work moves asynchronously, but a call is often the best way to start or to settle a decision. Mention it in your message and we'll pick a time in the hours our time zones share.

What happens to the details I send?

They're used only to reply to you and discuss your project. The form sends your message straight to me, and nothing is added to a mailing list. The privacy policy explains the details.
