06Guides · Plain English

Website guides, written for the people who run the business.

These are practical guides for business owners who are planning, building or improving a website.

I write each one from what I see in real projects: how AI search picks its sources, how to brief a designer, what to check before launch, how accessibility law applies. Every term gets explained, no statistic is invented, and every guide gives you concrete steps to act on yourself.

How often are the guides updated?

Whenever the facts they rely on change. Every guide shows its last-updated date under the title, and I revise a guide when a primary source it cites, such as Google Search Central or the W3C, publishes something that changes the advice.

Do I need technical knowledge to follow the guides?

No. They're written for people who run a business, not for developers. A technical term is explained the first time it appears, and the glossary covers the rest in one or two sentences each.

Can I ask for a guide on a topic that isn't here yet?

Yes. New guides start from questions people actually ask me, so send yours by email or WhatsApp. If it's useful to other business owners too, it goes on the list for a future guide.

Can I hire you to do what a guide describes?

Yes. Each guide links to the matching service, and the contact page takes a short brief. I reply within one business day with questions or a plan, and the work comes as a fixed project price or a monthly plan, whichever suits you.
