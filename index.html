How long does a project usually take?

Most landing pages take 1–2 weeks. Full marketing sites typically take 4–6 weeks, depending on scope, content readiness and integrations. Your proposal will include a clear timeline.

How do you price a project?

Projects are quoted at a fixed price based on the agreed scope. We discuss your goals first, then you receive a clear proposal with deliverables, timing and cost. Any scope changes are agreed together.

Can you refresh my existing website?

Absolutely. We start by understanding what already works and where the experience can improve. That might mean a focused refresh or a complete new direction, depending on your goals.

Will I be able to update the content?

If managing your own content is part of the brief, we choose a suitable CMS and build that into the project. The handoff includes guidance so you feel comfortable making updates.

What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of post-launch care. After that, we can discuss ongoing support or a lighter maintenance arrangement, depending on what your website needs.

Do you work with clients outside Israel?

Yes. I'm based in Tel Aviv and work with clients worldwide, in English and Hebrew. We can work asynchronously and schedule calls when a conversation will move things forward.
