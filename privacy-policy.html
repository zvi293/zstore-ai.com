What happens to the details you send through the contact form. Short, plain and complete.

  1. 01Introduction

    This Privacy Policy describes how Zvi Moshe ("I", "me", "my"), the person behind Zstore AI, collects, uses, and protects information submitted through the contact form on this website. By using this site, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

  2. 02Information we collect

    When you submit the contact form, the following information is collected:

    • Full Name — to address you properly.
    • Email Address — to respond to your inquiry.

    This data is submitted directly to a Google Apps Script endpoint and stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet.

  3. 03How we use information

    The information you provide is used solely to:

    • Respond to your inquiry or project request.
    • Communicate about potential collaboration.
    • Improve the quality of services offered.

  4. 04Data sharing

    Your personal information is never sold, rented, or traded to third parties. Information may be shared only when:

    • Required by law or legal process.
    • Necessary to protect rights or safety.

  5. 05Data retention

    Contact form submissions are retained for a reasonable period necessary to respond to inquiries and maintain business records. If you would like your data deleted, please contact me and I will remove it within a reasonable timeframe.

  7. 07Cookies & analytics

    This website does not currently use cookies or third-party analytics tools. If analytics are implemented in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.
