What is custom website design?

A website whose structure, design and code are made for one business. Nothing is chosen before your goals and content are clear; the layout follows them, not the other way around. I design every page from scratch and hand-code it to be fast, accessible and easy to find, on a fixed project price or a monthly plan, whichever suits you.

Weighing the options? My guide to choosing between a custom website or a website builder covers when each one makes sense, including when a builder is the right call.

Is custom website design right for my business?

It is when your website is a main source of leads or sales, when you need to look unlike your competitors, or when templates keep getting in the way. And sometimes it isn't, which is worth knowing before you spend anything.

Worth itwhen…

  • Your site brings in leads, bookings or sales, so every improvement shows up in the business.
  • You need to look and sound unlike the competitor one tab over.
  • You keep hitting the edges of a theme: a layout it can't do, a feature it can't add, pages that load slowly.
  • You want the design, code and content to be yours, with no platform deciding what's possible.

Not yetwhen…

  • You need a placeholder while the business takes shape.
  • It's a hobby or personal page with no commercial job to do.
  • You're testing an idea and need it live in days. A builder, or a single landing page, may be the smarter first step.

How long does a custom website take?

Typically 4–6 weeks for a full marketing site. A single landing page is usually 1–2 weeks. Your proposal comes with its own timeline, and the four-step process shows how a project moves from Discover to Launch.

  • Scope

    How many pages, page types and features the site needs.

  • Content

    Whether copy and images are ready, or written along the way.

  • Integrations

    Each connection to payments, booking, a CRM or a CMS adds build and test time.

Can I edit the site myself afterward?

Yes, when self-editing is part of the brief. We choose a CMS that suits how often you'll update and who will do it, and I design editable templates around your own content types. At handoff I walk you through it, so changing a headline or adding a page doesn't need me.

Is a custom website more work for me than a template?

A little more thinking at the start, much less wrestling later. I'll ask about your goals, audience and content in a discovery conversation, then do the design and build myself. Your main jobs are sharing what you know, providing or approving content, and giving clear feedback at each stage.

Do you design the site before coding it?

Yes. Type, color, structure and motion come together in a design and an interactive prototype you can click through on your phone and desktop. We refine the details together, and development starts only once the direction feels right to you.

What if I already have a brand identity?

Then the website should carry it faithfully. I work with your existing logo, colors and fonts and extend them for the screen: hierarchy, spacing, interaction and motion. If you don't have an identity yet, the creative direction step sets a clear visual direction for the site.

Can a custom website grow with my business later?

Yes. Hand-coded sites are built from reusable pieces, so adding pages, sections or features later is straightforward. If you expect frequent new pages, we plan for that from the start, for example with a CMS and templates designed for your own content types.

Do you build websites in languages other than English?

Yes. I work with clients in English and Hebrew, and I've built sites in other languages too, like the Spanish-language site in my selected work. Tell me in your brief which languages you need and we'll plan how translations will be supplied.
