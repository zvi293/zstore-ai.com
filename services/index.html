What does every web design and development project include?

The same constants, whatever the size. A one-page launch and a full online store get the same care in the parts you never see.

  • Designed from scratchNo templates or themes. Your layout starts on a blank page and takes its shape from your content.
  • Hand-codedI write the HTML, CSS and JavaScript myself. No page builders, no bundle of plugins.
  • No hand-offsThe person you brief designs your site, codes it and answers your messages.
  • Mobile-firstDesigned for a phone screen first, then shaped for tablets and large screens.
  • ~1.2s average loadLean code and optimized images. That's the average load time across my projects.
  • Accessible + SEO-readySemantic markup, keyboard support, readable contrast, metadata and structured data from the start.
  • A clear proposalScope, a timeline and a fixed project price, or a monthly plan if that suits you better.
  • 30 days of careAfter launch: fixes for anything that doesn't work as agreed, small adjustments and help getting comfortable.

PService · Product design

Digital product design UI/UX

Digital product design is the work behind an app, dashboard or tool: how people move through it, what each screen shows and how it feels to use.

I map the user journeys first, then design the interface screen by screen and turn it into an interactive prototype you can click through before anything is built. A design system (shared colors, type, components and rules) keeps every new screen consistent as the product grows.

IService · Integrations

Code and connections.

Code and connections is the wiring that makes a website do things: take payments, capture leads, update content and talk to the tools you already use. I build each connection by hand and test it end to end, so nothing breaks quietly after launch.

  • PaymentsSecure checkout through an established payment provider, which handles the card details.
  • FormsContact, quote and booking forms with spam protection, delivered where you'll see them.
  • Content managementA CMS when editing content yourself is part of the brief, with handoff guidance.
  • Email and CRMNew sign-ups and leads sent to the email or CRM tool you already use.
  • BookingsScheduling tools connected to the pages where people decide to book.
  • AnalyticsMeasurement set up so you can see which pages bring in leads and sales.

Selling online? See e-commerce websites. New to the terms? The glossary explains what a CMS is and how a payment gateway works.

KService · Refinement and care

Refinement and care

30 days included

Refinement and care keeps a live website fast, accessible and easy to find long after launch day.

Every launch includes 30 days of post-launch care: fixes for anything that doesn't work as agreed, small adjustments and help getting comfortable with your site. After that, an optional monthly Care plan can include:

  • Hosting, security and uptime monitoring, with backups and updates.
  • Small content changes each month, without opening a new project.
  • Health checks for performance, accessibility and SEO, with a short monthly report.
  • Priority replies when something needs attention.

You can add or remove services anytime; the plan changes from the next month.

Can you handle a project that mixes several services?

Yes, and most projects do. A new store often needs a landing page for launch, a few integrations and AI search foundations. Because one person handles design and code, combining services doesn't add coordination layers. The proposal lists every part with its own deliverables, under one fixed price based on the agreed scope.

Do you work with startups as well as established businesses?

Yes. I work with founders, small and mid-size businesses, e-commerce brands, professionals and startups. What matters more than company size is that the website has a clear job to do, whether that's testing an idea with a landing page or replacing a site a growing business has outgrown.

Do you only build new websites, or can you work on part of one?

Both. Besides full builds, I take on focused work on existing sites: a new section, an integration, a performance or accessibility pass, or AI search foundations. We start by looking at what's there, then agree on a scope that fixes the real problem without rebuilding what already works.

What does “hand-coded” mean for me as a client?

It means I write the HTML, CSS and JavaScript for your site myself instead of assembling it from a theme or a page builder. For you, that usually means a design that's genuinely yours, lean pages that load fast, and no bundle of plugins you didn't ask for. When you want to edit content yourself, I add a CMS on purpose.

Can you also design the product behind the website?

Yes. Digital product design (user journeys, interface design, interactive prototypes and design systems) is part of what I do. It pairs well with a marketing site, because the product and the website can share one visual language from the start.
